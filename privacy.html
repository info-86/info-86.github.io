Privacy Policy

True Self Quest LLC  ·  dba AtoddxMedia  ·  Last updated: June 2025

This policy explains what information we collect when you use this site, how we use it, and your rights as a California resident under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

What We Collect

We collect only what you type into the boxes. Nothing more. No cookies. No tracking. None of your information touches Google.

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the information you voluntarily provide: your name, email address, phone number (if given), and your responses to the form questions. We do not collect this information automatically — only when you choose to submit it.

Why We Collect It

We collect your information solely to respond to your inquiry and begin a conversation about coaching or personal navigation. We do not use it for advertising, marketing lists, or any automated decision-making.

How We Store It

Your form submission is sent directly to our email inbox at info@atoddx.com. We do not use a third-party database or CRM to store your information beyond that inbox.

We Do Not Sell Your Data

True Self Quest LLC dba AtoddxMedia does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third party for commercial purposes. Ever.

Your Rights Under California Law (CCPA)

As a California resident, you have the right to:

— Know what personal information we have collected about you.
— Request that we delete your personal information.
— Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell it).
— Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@atoddx.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 45 days.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any analytics software.

Children

This site is not directed at anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised date at the top of this page will reflect the change. We will not retroactively change how we handle information already submitted.
